Station names are written differently across the three lists (for example "ATP I TOWN", "ATP 1 Town PS"); they are grouped under one name here, and the name exactly as printed is shown in each person's details. In the HC list, seniority no. 228 is missing, so serial and seniority numbers differ by one from Sl. 228 on. A few entries are printed as in the source even where they look like typing errors (a joining date of 19-01-1900, a blank date of birth, a category written as "0" or "Nil"); these are flagged in the person's details. Retirement is worked out as the end of the month the person turns 62, for reference only.
